# Inbox & contacts

## Inbox

- **Conversation list** with contact/group names, pictures, previews, timestamps, unread counts, pinned and archived markers.
- **Filters:** all, unread, individuals, groups, archived, and by account.
- **Search** across names, numbers, group names and locally synchronised message text.
- **Conversation view:** sender names in groups, delivery and read ticks, quoted replies, images, video, documents, audio and voice notes, stickers, reactions, locations, edits and deletions.
- **Composer:** text, emoji, attachments (send as media or document), replies, reactions, drafts saved automatically, and **Schedule**.
- Everything updates live without refreshing.

### History and sync state

Chinwag can only store what WhatsApp Web makes available. Each chat shows whether it is **fully** synchronised, **partially** synchronised (older messages may exist on the phone) or not synchronised. Use **Load older from WhatsApp** at the top of a conversation to fetch more.

### Media

Incoming media is downloaded automatically (configurable) into private storage and served only to users with access to that account. Large or old media can be fetched on demand.

## Contacts

Each account has its own directory — contacts are never merged across accounts. Search by name, number or tag; add **tags** and internal **notes** (never sent to the contact); open the related conversation.
